## Bottom line

Raw FASTQ -> our commands reproduces the Plasmidsaurus portal outputs
essentially exactly for all 4 samples.

Before the fix (v1), we were 1.4-2% low on mapped reads and counts, with
81-83% of genes exact.

## What fixed it

fastp 3' quality trimming. The portal uses `--cut_tail` at fastp defaults
(window 4, mean Q20), not mean Q15:

    fastp --trim_poly_x --cut_tail --qualified_quality_phred 15 --length_required 50

Checked three ways: the fastp read count equals the portal's STAR input
(19,715,741); the trimmed length is identical for 100% of reads (compared
against the reads recoverable from the portal's mapped and unmapped BAMs);
and STAR unique/multi counts match exactly.

## Remaining residual (probably not fixable, and negligible)

UMI dedup differs by 3-20 reads per sample, only among multi-mappers, so
about 0.1% of genes are off by a fraction of a read. Likely cause:
UMICollapse's choice of which duplicate to keep when reads tie. Not yet
investigated.

## Exact recipe (all verified)

- Reference: Ensembl GRCm39 release 114 primary assembly + ERCC92
  (`data/reference/`); @SQ matches the portal exactly.
- fastp 0.24.0: as above.
- STAR 2.7.11b: defaults + `--outFilterIntronMotifs RemoveNoncanonical
  --outSAMattributes NH HI AS nM NM MD --outSAMtype BAM Unsorted
  --outReadsUnmapped Fastx`, then `samtools sort`.
- UMICollapse: `bam --umi-sep _` (UMI follows the last `_` in the read name).
- featureCounts (subread 2.1.1): `-t gene -g gene_id -s 1 -M --fraction`.
  CPM = count / total * 1e6.
